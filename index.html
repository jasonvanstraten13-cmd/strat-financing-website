VAT

VAT201 figures you can type straight into eFiling.

Every VAT amount is recorded in the period it belongs to. Once you've filed, lock the period, and the return you submitted stays exactly as it was.

  • Standard, zero-rated, exempt and no VAT on every line, at 15%.
  • Voids and credit notes count in the period they happen, like they should.
  • Equipment purchases go to field 14 as capital goods.
  • Not VAT registered? New lines default to No VAT, and documents say Invoice, not Tax Invoice.

Switching from Xero or another system

Move over on a date you choose.

You need two reports from your current system on the conversion date: the trial balance and your list of unpaid invoices and bills.

  1. Pick a conversion date

    Month-end works best. The start of your financial year is cleanest.

  2. Enter your opening balances

    One journal for bank, equipment, loans and equity, straight from the trial balance.

  3. Add what's still owed

    Each unpaid invoice and bill goes in as an opening balance, so payments match against it.

  4. Add stock and lock the old period

    Enter opening stock per product, then lock everything before the conversion date.

Questions

Before you sign up.

Is Strat Finances approved by SARS?

SARS doesn't approve accounting software. Strat Finances works out the figures for your VAT201, and you capture them on eFiling yourself. Check them with your tax practitioner before you file.

Can my accountant work in my books?

Yes. Add them with the accountant role at no charge. They can do everything except manage people, backups and the lock date. You can also add view-only users.

Which banks does it work with?

Any bank that lets you download a CSV statement with a date and an amount, or debit and credit columns. Common South African layouts, including Dr and Cr markers and amounts like 1 234,56, are read as they are.

How is my data protected?

Passwords are hashed, accounts lock for 15 minutes after five wrong attempts, and every change and sign-in is recorded in the activity log. You can download a full backup at any time and have one emailed to you every Monday.

Can I take my data with me if I leave?

Yes. Download a complete backup of your books, including attachments, and export your invoices as a CSV file.

Does it do payroll?

Not yet. Keep using your payroll provider and code the salary payments from your bank statement to the Salaries account.
